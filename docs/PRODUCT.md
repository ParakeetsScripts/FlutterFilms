# FlutterFilms by Ritvik M — Product

## Tagline

**Your movies. Your taste. Your AI.**

## Problem

"I don't know what to watch."

## Solution

Movie data + taste + AI reasoning + discovery + planning + social intelligence.

## Primary navigation

Home · Discover · AI · My Cinema · Watchlist · Community

## Flagship experiences

1. **Home hero** — natural language → real catalog results
2. **Tonight** (`/tonight`) — one primary recommendation with explanation
3. **Cinema Agent** (`/copilot`) — tool-grounded AI
4. **Taste DNA** (`/my-cinema`) — visualized taste signals from real data
5. **Scene Memory** (`/intelligence/remember`) — describe a movie, get possible matches

## Growth loops

- Shareable Taste DNA, lists, movie-night cards, room invites
- CTA on shared pages: **Discover your movies on FlutterFilms by Ritvik M**

## Monetization foundation

Configurable tiers (Free / Pro / Teams) with usage limits for AI and generation — pricing not hardcoded.

## Investor demo

`/demo` — labeled **DEMO ACCOUNT**, real catalog, no fake analytics.
